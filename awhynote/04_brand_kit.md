# awhynote: Brand Kit

## 1. Identity
- **Name:** awhynote (read as "a why note"): a short note that answers a "why".
- **Tagline:** "Everything has a why." (alts: "The why behind everyday things." / "Small notes. Big whys.")
- **Mission:** Explain the hidden history, design and tech of everyday things in under 60 seconds. India first, built for the world.
- **Voice:** a curious friend who happens to be a good teacher. Short sentences, one idea per line, no hype. Say "reportedly" when unsure. Always cite.
- **Language:** simple English. Decide once whether captions allow light Hinglish, and then stay consistent.

Rule: at most 3 colours on screen at once (Ink + Paper + Yellow by default).

## 4. Typography (all free, Google Fonts, OFL)
- **Headlines / captions:** Poppins ExtraBold (pipeline default; has the ₹ glyph). Alternatives built in: Montserrat ExtraBold, Anton (yellow-text highlight), Bebas Neue. Preview with `awhynote styles`. Archivo Black was dropped: it has no ₹.
- **Accent words (serif italic):** Instrument Serif Italic or Cormorant Garamond Italic.
- **Tags, locations, sources:** JetBrains Mono or Space Mono, all caps, wide tracking.

## 5. Video template (Reels / Shorts)
- **Canvas:** 1080×1920, 30 fps, H.264, 45–60 s (aim for 45 s).
- **Safe zone:** keep captions and key graphics roughly between y=250 and y=1500, and 65 px in from the sides. Confirm in Instagram's in-app preview.
- **Captions:** 2–4 words at a time, about 64–72 px, centred, one yellow highlight box on the key word.
- **Series tag (top-left, mono, 28 px):** `AWHYNOTE · NOTE 001`.
- **Handle watermark:** small, top-right, 50% opacity.
- **Location/date chips:** `| KONARK · ODISHA`, `| 1938`, mono caps, in a dark pill.
- **Year stamp:** big yellow torn-paper shape with the year in black.
- **Intro:** a 0.5 s sound sting, with no logo animation. The hook starts at frame 1.
- **Outro (last 4 s):** one question to the viewer (drives comments), a teaser of the next episode, `SOURCE: …`, and the sign-off line **"EVERYTHING HAS A WHY."**
- **Hook-first rule:** the first spoken line is the `first_3s_hook` from the backlog, and the payoff is the `twist_payoff`. Don't spend the first 3 s on a logo or intro.

### Visual rule: hybrid documentary
Real photographs, archival images and licensed footage are the foundation. AI is used to animate selected images, build maps and diagrams, and fill gaps. Never use AI for realistic banknotes, real historical people's faces, or anything presented as a real photo.

## 6. Visual types (rotate every 2–3 s)
1. **Archival / vintage photo:** sepia, vignette, slight paper border, with a callout.
2. **Macro of the real object:** your own photo or scan, with a red circle and a white hand-drawn arrow.
3. **Map with a pin:** a minimal paper-toned map, a yellow dot, a label in mono.
4. **Stylised illustration:** flat paper-cut style in the palette.
5. **Stat card:** a big number plus a 3-word label.
6. **Licensed stock b-roll:** graded to match (vignette, 8% grain).

**Motion:** slow push-in or pan (5–8% scale), hard cuts, with a quick whip only on a reveal. Vignette 30%, grain 8%.

## 7. Audio
- **Voice:** calm, measured, about 150 wpm. Keep one voice for the whole channel.
- **Music:** low-key ambient, 15–20 dB below the voice. Use only the Instagram library or royalty-free tracks.
- **Loudness:** master at about -14 LUFS with true peak under -1 dB.

## 8. Cover and grid
- Design at 1080×1920, but keep the title inside the centre 3:4 area so it survives the grid crop.
- Big title of 6 words or fewer, one yellow highlight word, and the series number (e.g. `NOTE 001`).
- Use 3 cover tints for the grid: Money = brown, History = sepia, Tech = steel-blue. Keep Ink and Yellow constant.

## 9. Editorial standards (the trust layer)
1. **Two-source rule:** every claim needs at least two independent sources, and one should be a primary source (RBI, ECB, museum, archive).
2. **Uncertain claims:** label legends and theories as such ("reportedly", "one theory").
3. **On-screen source line** in the last 3 s, plus a pinned comment with links.
4. **Corrections:** pin a correction comment and update the caption within 24 h.
5. **Currency imagery:**
   - Use only photos or scans of the real note that you own.
   - Never generate realistic replica notes with AI.
   - Check each country's reproduction rules before using note images.
   - Cover only publicly published security features.
6. **AI disclosure:** switch on Instagram's AI label for realistic AI imagery or voices.
7. **Images:** public domain, Creative Commons (with attribution), your own, or licensed. Don't lift images from random websites.
